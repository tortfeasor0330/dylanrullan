What you'll learn

01

Nitric oxide: the molecule that keeps blood vessels relaxed

Learn what nitric oxide does inside your blood vessels, why its levels tend to drop with age and lifestyle, and which daily habits help your body make more of it.

🏅 Its role in blood vessels won the 1998 Nobel Prize in Medicine

Listen to your body

Does any of this sound familiar?

These can be signs that your circulation needs attention. In the webinar, Dr. Rullan explains what they can mean and what you can do about them.

These signs can have many causes. If you notice them, please have them checked by a doctor.

🖐️Cold hands or feet
⚡Tingling or numbness
🦵Leg cramps when walking
💧Swollen ankles or feet
😮‍💨Tiring easily
🩹Wounds that heal slowly
🧠Brain fog or dizziness
🩺High blood pressure readings
