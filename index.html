About the Doctor

More Than a Prescription

Dr. Dylan Thor P. Rullan practises integrative and naturopathic medicine: evidence-based conventional care, combined with nutrition, botanical medicine and lifestyle therapy. The aim is simple. Find out why you feel unwell, then treat that.

A first consultation runs a full hour. Your history, your diet, your sleep and your stress all count as clinical information, and you leave with a written plan rather than a rushed note.

  • Root-Cause AssessmentDetailed history, physical exam and targeted labs
  • Natural & Conventional TogetherBotanicals and nutrition that respect your current medication
  • Follow-Up You Can ReachScheduled reviews to adjust the plan as you improve
