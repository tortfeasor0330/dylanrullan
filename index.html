About the Doctor

Dr. Dylan Thor Pablo R. Rullan

Doctor of Integrative MedicineDoctor of NaturopathyPhD in NaturopathyDoctor of HumanitiesChiropractic Care PractitionerHealth Educator

Dr. Dylan Thor Pablo R. Rullan is a Doctor of Integrative Medicine, Naturopathic Doctor in Advanced Diploma, Doctor of Humanities, and holder of a PhD in Naturopathy, dedicated to advancing integrative, preventive, and holistic approaches to health and wellness.

A cum laude graduate, Dr. Rullan has developed a multidisciplinary educational foundation that includes a Bachelor's Degree in Political Science, with academic units in Master's in Educational Management, complemented by specialized training and certifications in areas related to integrative health, including Iridology and Chiropractic Care.

With extensive hands-on experience in chiropractic care and integrative wellness, Dr. Rullan believes that effective health care should look at the individual as a whole: physical condition, nutrition, lifestyle, habits, environment, and overall well-being.

He is particularly passionate about naturopathy, lifestyle wellness, nutrition and food intake, preventive health, chiropractic care, and health education. He has also developed a non-invasive eye-cleanse procedure focused on the external cleansing of the eyes and removal of accumulated dirt, dust, and other external debris, with the goal of supporting clearer visual comfort. His work also includes educational wellness programs addressing eye health and diabetic retinopathy; he emphasizes that such programs are complementary and should not replace appropriate ophthalmologic care.

Over the years, Dr. Rullan has received numerous testimonials from clients who attribute significant improvements in their health and quality of life to the care, guidance, and wellness programs they received. These stories have strengthened his commitment to helping people pursue healthier lifestyles while encouraging responsible, informed health decisions.

Beyond clinical and academic work, Dr. Rullan is known for his passion for teaching, mentoring, community education, and public speaking. He has appeared as a guest on various radio programs and local television channels, sharing his knowledge on health, wellness, lifestyle, and practical approaches to improving one's quality of life.

He is also known by many as “Master Lance,” a name associated with his knowledge and practice of Feng Shui and his interest in helping people create environments and lifestyles that promote harmony, balance, and positive living.

A lifelong learner and advocate of continuous personal development, Dr. Rullan believes that knowledge becomes more meaningful when it is shared.

Outside his professional work, Dr. Rullan enjoys dancing and traveling, reflecting his belief that a healthy life should also be an active, joyful, and meaningful one.

Today, through his continuing work in integrative medicine, naturopathy, wellness education, and community health advocacy, Dr. Dylan Thor Pablo R. Rullan remains committed to one purpose: empowering people with knowledge and practical tools to take a more active role in their health and well-being.
