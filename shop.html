Frequently Asked

Can I take the Triangle with my medication?
Check with your doctor first, especially if you take blood thinners, diabetes or blood-pressure medication, or are pregnant or breastfeeding. You can book a consultation with Dr. Rullan to review your current medication.
Why take Nitro before the other two?
Nitro is designed to support nitric oxide production, which helps with nutrient delivery. Taking it first prepares your body for Sunrise in the morning and Sunset in the evening.
When will I notice a difference?
Everyone is different. Nutritional support works best taken consistently, which is why we suggest at least one full month alongside a balanced diet and regular activity.
